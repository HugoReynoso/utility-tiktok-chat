A free TikFinity alternative for reading your LIVE chat aloud

TikFinity is a very complete toolkit for TikTok LIVE. If what you mainly need is to have the chat read aloud, plus a few sound alerts, TikTok Chat does that for free, in the browser and with no sign-up.

When TikTok Chat is enough

  • You just want to hear the chat while you are live.
  • You do not want subscriptions or paid limits.
  • You would rather not install anything or create an account.
  • You want simple filters: followers, subscribers, keywords and words to skip.

When TikFinity is the better choice

  • You need graphic overlays to show in OBS or LIVE Studio.
  • You want a wider set of automatic actions and events.
